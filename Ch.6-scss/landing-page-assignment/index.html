Meet The Artists!

--Mina--

Owner and resident artist

Ever since the age of 9, I would watch anime, draw and listen to nightcore for hours upon hours after school.
The media that inspired me was Japanese animation such as Sailor Moon, Pokémon, Digimon, Sonic the Hedgehog, and Tokyo Mew Mew.
My interests have absolutely carried over into my tattoos and designs and I ensure that every shape is from my own hand.
No idea has ever been silly to me, or boring, because there are things about EVERY tattoo I do that I love and enjoy. Even the simplest of designs.

Consider me for your next tattoo! Lets get dorky!
