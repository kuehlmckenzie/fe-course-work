How To Train Your Dragon

A three part childrens animated triology, seen as arguably the best animated series in this generation.

As proven by the 10 Annie awards won by the first movie alone. some of which include "Best Animated Feature" and "Best Writing".

Introduction to the Isle of Berk

Inside the universe of How to Train Your Dragon takes place mainly in a remote island based off an isle off the coast of Scotland. In the first movie, the misery of life on Berk is emphasized by it's descripted from the point of view of Hiccup, the main protagonist, with phrases such as "tweleve days north of Hopeless and a few degrees south of Freezing to Death on the Merdian of Misery ". Which becomes a very stark difference of the harmony and chaos seen on Berk after the events of the first movie.
