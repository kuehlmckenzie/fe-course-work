A World Full Of Dragons

One of the many traits that makes Berk so interesting is the fact that they don't hunt dragons, they RIDE them.

Throughout the universe of HTTYD dragons fly across the sky, swim the depths below, and walk among the trees. We are introduced to many species alongside main characters.

The dragons the series tends to focus on are the ones commonly ridden by their human counterparts. Down below there is a table featuring those main dragons.
